## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are students — engineering and CSE students, developers, Linux users, gamers, and productivity enthusiasts — whose laptops are bogged down by bloatware, slow startups, clutter, and broken or missing setups. Their job: get a single laptop that is fast, reliable, and ready for study, coding, and gaming without hours of setup.

## Product Purpose

Project NOVA is a student-focused laptop transformation service. It debloats and optimizes Windows, installs or dual-boots Linux, configures complete developer environments, tunes gaming setups, and sets up productivity systems — so one laptop serves all of a student's life. Success is a fully transformed, ready-to-use machine delivered in a single booking.

## Positioning

All-in-one laptop transformation. One service bundles what other shops do piecemeal: optimization, OS setup, developer tooling, gaming configuration, and productivity systems. The customer gets a complete outcome instead of a menu of one-off fixes.

## Operating Context

- Primarily in-person: customers bring their laptops to the shop for service, especially for complex work like dual boot or hardware diagnostics.
- Remote sessions via TeamViewer/AnyDesk are available for software-only services (debloat, optimization, software install) with a stable internet connection on the customer's end.
- Customers are responsible for backing up their own data; backup help can be sold as an additional service.
- Service durations: 30 min–1 hr (quick), 1–2 hrs (advanced), 2–4 hrs (complex such as dual boot or full transformation).
- Bookings through the site's contact form; follow-up via email (projectnovaservices@gmail.com), phone/WhatsApp (9704149889), and Instagram.
- Built for students, by students; pricing is student-affordable, with occasional discounts and referral programs.
- Custom service requests are evaluated with a quote and timeline before work starts; asking is free.
- Specific service location/venue and coverage area are not established in the repo or by the owner.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Capabilities:
- Windows debloat and performance optimization (startup, services, storage, driver stability)
- Fresh Windows installation with drivers, updates, and a secured restore point
- Linux installation, dual boot, and virtual machines (Ubuntu, Mint, Fedora, Manjaro)
- Developer environment setup: VS Code, Git/GitHub SSH, Python, Java, C/C++, Android Studio, Node.js, Docker, Ollama
- Obsidian vault configuration with cloud sync across devices
- Gaming configuration: launchers, GPU drivers, per-game profiles, FPS tuning
- Custom services outside the listed menu

Constraints:
- OS-level work (reinstalls, repartitioning, registry/update changes) carries inherent data-loss risk; backup is the customer's responsibility and is required.
- Windows updates can revert customizations after the service; update-related fixes may incur a separate charge after the service period.
- Satisfaction issues reported within 48 hours are addressed at no extra cost; refunds are evaluated case by case.
- The site itself is a Next.js (React + TypeScript + Tailwind) codebase; the current visual theme is a dynamic, palette-driven HSL system documented in docs/DESIGN.md and tuned by the owner.

Terminology: NOVA = Notebook Optimization, Virtualization & Advancement.

## Product Principles

1. The student is the user — every capability serves study, development, or gaming life.
2. Transform, don't just fix — deliver a complete, ready-to-use setup, not a one-off tweak.
3. Safety first — backups, restore points, and verified completion before any system-level change.
4. Transparent and honest — clear quotes, timelines, and risk disclosure; no surprise charges.
5. Real technical depth — the service is backed by genuine knowledge of Linux, developer tooling, and gaming, and it should feel that way.
